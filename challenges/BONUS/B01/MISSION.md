# B01 · Simon Says: Dispatch Edition

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Interactive game  
**Location:** Dispatch Desk · **Focus:** Memory game + keyed XOR

## Mission briefing
The night dispatcher will only speak in sequences. Repeat the call-backs cleanly and the desk coughs up an access key; without it the tape you pulled is just noise.

## Objective
Beat the dispatch memory game to earn the key, then unmask the tape. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `dispatch_tape.txt`

## Interactive component
This mission ships a browser mini-game hosted on the event site. Open the mission card on the site to play it; the download alone is not enough to reach the key.

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
