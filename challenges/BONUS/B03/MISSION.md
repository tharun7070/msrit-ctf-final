# B03 · Captain's Log, Star Date Whatever

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Interactive game  
**Location:** Bridge Terminal · **Focus:** Command-parsing game + keyed XOR

## Mission briefing
The bridge terminal only listens to a captain who knows the ship. Talk it into unlocking the log and it prints the key to the manifest.

## Objective
Issue the right commands to the in-browser terminal and decode the manifest. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `starship_manifest.txt`

## Interactive component
This mission ships a browser mini-game hosted on the event site. Open the mission card on the site to play it; the download alone is not enough to reach the key.

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
