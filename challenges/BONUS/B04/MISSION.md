# B04 · Achievement Unlocked: Touched Grass

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Interactive game  
**Location:** The Outside · **Focus:** Patience/idle game + keyed XOR

## Mission briefing
Every gamer knows the rarest achievement there is: log off and go outside. The launcher refuses to reveal anything until you do precisely nothing for a while.

## Objective
Earn the achievement, then decode the launcher manifest. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `achievements.json`

## Interactive component
This mission ships a browser mini-game hosted on the event site. Open the mission card on the site to play it; the download alone is not enough to reach the key.

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
