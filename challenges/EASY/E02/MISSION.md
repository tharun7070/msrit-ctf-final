# E02 · Vinewood Shift Change

**Level:** Easy · **Points:** 50 · **Category:** Cryptography  
**Location:** Vinewood Boulevard · **Focus:** Caesar cipher

## Mission briefing
A taxi ledger contains three intercepted messages. The wanted driver used the route marked MIDNIGHT, with a wheel count and a spare wheel hint. Recover that message using a Caesar rotation.

## Objective
Investigate the attached evidence. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FLAG{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `taxi_ledger.txt`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
