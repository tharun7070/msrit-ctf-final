# E02 · Vinewood Shift Change

**Level:** Easy · **Points:** 50 · **Category:** Cryptography  
**Location:** Vinewood Boulevard · **Focus:** Affine cipher + checksum filtering

## Mission briefing
Vinewood's night-shift taxi ledger is mostly legitimate fares. One car was never supposed to be logged, and its manifest line is encrypted with a shift nobody wrote down. A decoy line decrypts perfectly under the lazy assumption — and leads nowhere.

## Objective
Find the genuine intercepted line, break its cipher, and recover the manifest. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `taxi_ledger.txt`
- `frequency_sample.bin`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
