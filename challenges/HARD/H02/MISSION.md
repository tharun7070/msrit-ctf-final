# H02 · The Recursive Freeway

**Level:** Hard · **Points:** 160 · **Category:** Algorithms  
**Location:** Final Getaway · **Focus:** Weighted graph isomorphism + spectral fingerprint

## Mission briefing
The getaway routing grid is a weighted graph in which every junction looks alike, so degree filtering is useless. Somewhere inside sits a single district that matches a stolen weighted blueprint, and several near-miss districts survive a first look. The district's registry scraps are stored on its junctions in no trustworthy order. Only the right weighted invariants single out the true district, and only the right order reassembles the token.

## Objective
Find the unique weight-consistent 12-junction district and assemble its registry fragments into the clearance flag exactly as PROTOCOL.md specifies. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

- `PROTOCOL.md`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
