MAZE BANK OF LOS SANTOS - SECURITY AUDIT BRIEF (case ref: H04)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The token service shipped as bank_gateway.py was pushed to production
in a hurry. During a routine sweep the audit crew noticed a leftover
debug route (/sign_debug) that is still wired up in the local build,
and a quieter one (/verify_debug) nobody remembers approving.

We recovered one working credential off a teller's shared drive:

    guest.token

It is only good for read-only teller traffic. The vault order the bank
actually cares about is signed under a different role and carries a
rotating audit nonce; the gateway will only hand over the registered
marker when a correctly signed order is presented together with a
fresh proof of that same nonce.

Two leads the night crew scribbled down - treat them as rumours, not
facts, because the shift that wrote them had been drinking:

  * "The debug route corrupts its answer about 10% of the time, and
     whenever it does it is always the p-side half."
  * "The order the vault honours is the legacy string with
     NONCE=LS2026; the stale order pinned in the build still works."

Ask the debug route to sign reference traffic and compare what it hands
back across a few requests before you trust any single answer - the raw
answer is not the signature it looks like. The quiet route answers
questions about the audit nonce slowly, one byte at a time.
