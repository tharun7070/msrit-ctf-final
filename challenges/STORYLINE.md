# FLAG THEFT AUTO — The Los Santos Files

## 00:00 — The Phone Rings

A radio crackles inside an abandoned garage on the edge of Los Santos. The city's digital underworld is in chaos: a rogue dispatch network has hidden fifteen clearance fragments across police case files, airport transmissions, black-market packages, and an abandoned military gate. The elusive Broker has contracted your crew to recover the fragments before the citywide network lockdown.

Your crew is **not** breaking into real banks or computers. This is a fictional, offline evidence hunt: every mission has a self-contained, authorized digital artifact, and the only heist is against a puzzle.

The Broker's last instruction is simple: **“Bring me the flags. Leave every real system untouched.”**

## Act I — The Street Job

Your getaway car needs a route. First you must piece together Lester's intercepted pager, decode a Vinewood taxi message, inspect a hidden safehouse stash, read the Del Perro camera record, and discover a false-file-extension trick at Los Santos Customs. These five street missions introduce cryptography and evidence inspection. Each successful extraction earns **50 points**.

*Radio call:* “The city is wide open. Start small. The easy money is in the details nobody checks.”

## Act II — The Big Heist

The crew's operation grows more dangerous—but remains completely fictional. Use a local mock Fleeca Bank kiosk with intentionally faulty SQL, trace a ghost aircraft's HTTP dispatch in a captured airport PCAP, reconstruct an LSPD evidence log, decrypt an Underground Market radio message, and repair a damaged Python courier. Five middle-tier missions award **100 points** each.

*Radio call:* “Every camera, every packet, every line of code tells a different story. Find the one that's true.”

## Act III — The Final Getaway

The fictional authorities have closed the freeways. Crack a Fort Zancudo Linux gate binary, calculate a recursive freeway clearance, peel layers from an impound-camera archive, review a deliberately unsafe local authorization gateway, and decrypt the final escape transmission. Five high-tier missions award **160 points** each. Each challenge is independent: you never need flags from previous missions to complete a later one.

*Radio call:* “Forget the wanted stars. The only thing that matters now is your score. Get in, find the flags, get out.”

## Victory Condition

Recover as many of the fifteen unique `FLAG{...}` messages as possible, submit them to CTFd under your crew's account before the public deadline, and climb the leaderboard. Full completion totals **1,550 points**. Every crew has access to three free, progressive hints per mission.

**This is an original educational cyber-noir storyline inspired by open-world crime-game aesthetics, not affiliated with the creators of Grand Theft Auto.**
