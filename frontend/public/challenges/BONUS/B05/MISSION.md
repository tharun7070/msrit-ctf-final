# B05 · Niko's Pizza Receipt

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Misc  
**Location:** Pizza This · **Focus:** Receipt arithmetic + keyed XOR

## Mission briefing
Niko swears the pizza place skimmed him. The receipt adds up to nothing sensible and there is a blob stapled to the bottom; work out by how much he was fleeced and you can unwrap it.

## Objective
Find the overcharge on the itemised receipt and unwrap the blob. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `receipt.txt`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
