# B07 · Rick Roll Protocol

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Cryptography  
**Location:** The Internet · **Focus:** Lyric-keyed Vigenere + rail fence

## Mission briefing
A courier left a love letter that is 100 percent definitely not a rickroll. Decode it properly and it will tell you the rules, and then hand you the flag.

## Objective
Unwind the two cipher layers and recover the flag. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `lyrics_cipher.txt`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
