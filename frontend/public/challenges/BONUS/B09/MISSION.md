# B09 · Facebook Marketplace Rugpull

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** OSINT  
**Location:** Marketplace · **Focus:** Listing JSON + chat-price XOR

## Mission briefing
A 'cheap car, no lowballers, I know what I have' listing goes sideways. The listing and the seller's own chat log disagree; the price you were actually quoted is the key.

## Objective
Reconcile the listing with the chat and unwrap the blob. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `listing.json`
- `chat_log.txt`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
