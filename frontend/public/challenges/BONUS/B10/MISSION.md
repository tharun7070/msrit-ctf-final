# B10 · Grand Theft Alpaca: Install Linux

**Level:** Bonus · **Points:** 25 · **Category:** Reverse engineering  
**Location:** A Basement · **Focus:** Obfuscated shell script + base64

## Mission briefing
To claim the alpaca you must prove you can daily-drive a Linux desktop. The so-called installer is 90 percent jokes and 10 percent payload; run the logic in your head and it hands over the flag.

## Objective
Read the script like a machine and recover the embedded payload. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `installer_script.sh`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
