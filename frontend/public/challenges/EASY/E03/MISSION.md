# E03 · Sandy Shores Hidden Stash

**Level:** Easy · **Points:** 50 · **Category:** Linux forensics  
**Location:** Sandy Shores · **Focus:** ext2 image + deleted-file recovery

## Mission briefing
The Sandy Shores safehouse yielded a dead laptop, imaged before the drive could be wiped. The file you want is already deleted — but nothing is truly gone until the blocks are reused. A dock worker's log survives on the image and is more useful than it looks.

## Objective
Recover the deleted payload from the disk image and reverse its masking. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `safehouse.img`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
