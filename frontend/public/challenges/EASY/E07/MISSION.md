# E07 · Wanted Poster

**Level:** Easy · **Points:** 50 · **Category:** Steganography  
**Location:** Vinewood Hills · **Focus:** Hue-keyed Vigenere over a rail fence

## Mission briefing
A wanted poster from Vinewood Hills carries a prisoner's parting note and a row of printer's seal swatches that most people take for decoration. The ciphertext is printed in plain sight, but the watchword the note advertises is a plant; the real keyword is carried only by the colours of the seal. Two classical ciphers are stacked, and the poster also hides a lazy low-bit payload that decodes to a flag that will get you laughed out of the precinct.

## Objective
Recover the real keyword from the seal, unwind both classical layers, and submit the recovered flag. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

- `DEPUTIZE.txt`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
