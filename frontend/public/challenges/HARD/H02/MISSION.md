# H02 · The Recursive Freeway

**Level:** Hard · **Points:** 160 · **Category:** Algorithms  
**Location:** Final Getaway · **Focus:** Weighted graph isomorphism + spectral fingerprint

## Mission briefing
The getaway routing grid is a weighted graph in which every junction looks alike — degrees are useless as a filter. Somewhere inside sits a district that matches a stolen blueprint exactly, and several near-miss districts differ from it by a single edge or a single weight. Only the weighted spectral signature decides, and the blueprint's labels are read in an order that is not the node index. Brute force will not finish in time.

## Objective
Find the unique weight-consistent matching district and derive the clearance flag from it exactly as PROTOCOL.md specifies. Submit exactly one recovered flag to CTFd, in the form `FTA{...}`. The braces and capitalization matter. Do not submit explanations.

## Evidence files
- `junctions.json`
- `template.json`
- `PROTOCOL.md`

## Scope and safety
Work only on the downloaded challenge artifacts. All targets, identities and addresses are fictional. No scanning, account access, or attacks against a real network are required or allowed.

Hints are unlockable on the event platform; they are not required to solve the mission.
